## Available notebooks

### Chest X-ray Classifier — 30 minutes

Train a real deep-learning model on real chest X-rays. Uses transfer learning with a pre-trained ResNet-18 on a 500-image balanced subset of the NIH ChestX-ray14 dataset. Generates a shareable certificate with your final AUC.
